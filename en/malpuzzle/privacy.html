2. Overview

This privacy policy explains how personal data is handled when you use the Malpuzzle app for iPhone and iPad. Personal data is any information that can identify you personally (Art. 4(1) GDPR).

In short: Malpuzzle runs entirely on your device. I do not receive any data about you.

3. What data the app processes

Malpuzzle does not collect any personal data and does not transmit any data to me or to third parties. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking services. There are no in-app purchases and no Game Center integration. All data stays on your device.

Photos and camera: If you want to color one of your own photos, the app asks for access to your photos and your camera. The photo is processed only on your device and is never uploaded. You can revoke access at any time in the iOS Settings.

4. Download via the Apple App Store

The app is distributed via the Apple App Store. When you download, buy or update it, Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland) processes data such as your Apple Account, purchase information and device data under its own responsibility. I have no influence on this. For details, see Apple’s Privacy Policy.

If you have enabled “Share with App Developers” in the iOS Settings, Apple provides me with anonymized crash and usage statistics. They do not allow any conclusions about you as a person.

5. Your rights

Where I process personal data about you – for example when you send me an email – you have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR). Just send me an email.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the Thuringian State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (TLfDI).

6. Contact

Questions about privacy: rocconaut@icloud.com

If you send me an email, I process your email address and the content of your message only to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in answering requests) or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract. I delete your message once the request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. I use Apple iCloud Mail for email (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland).

7. Changes

I will update this privacy policy if the app or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
