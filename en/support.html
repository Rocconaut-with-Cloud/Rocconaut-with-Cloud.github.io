Color Puzzle – FAQ.

On the start screen, tap “Color a photo”. Then pick a photo from your library or take a new one with the camera. Color Puzzle turns it into a color-by-number picture.

There are three levels: Easy with fewer than 30 fields, Medium with more than 50 fields and Expert with 100–200 fields. For young children, “Easy” is ideal – the large fields are easy to tap.

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap Apps and then Color Puzzle. There you can allow or revoke access to photos and camera.
