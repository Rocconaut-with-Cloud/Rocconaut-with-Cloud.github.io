2. In short

Trail-Analyzer stores all data exclusively on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no cloud, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. I, as the developer, receive none of your data.

3. Location

During a recording, the app uses GPS to record your route, distance and speed. This also works with the screen locked, so your whole ride is recorded. Outside of a recording, your location is not used. You can change access at any time in the iOS Settings.

4. Motion sensors and barometer

The accelerometer, gyroscope and barometer are used during recording to calculate lean angle, g-forces, jumps and elevation gain. The values are processed only on your iPhone.

5. Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads your heart rate from Apple Health, only for the time of your rides. The app doesn’t write any data to Apple Health. Health data is never shared or used for advertising.

You grant access explicitly in iOS and can revoke it at any time in the Health app.

6. Maps

The app uses Apple Maps for the map view and the video export. Your iPhone loads the required map sections from Apple. Your recorded rides are not transmitted to Apple. For details, see Apple’s Privacy Policy.

7. Photos

The app only asks for access to your photo library when you want to save an exported video there. It can only add videos and doesn’t read any photos.

8. Sharing

Data only leaves your iPhone when you share a video yourself using the share menu. What happens to the video after that is subject to the terms of the app or person you share it with.

9. Storage and deletion

Your rides are stored locally in the app. You can delete individual rides or all rides in the app. Deleting the app removes all data.

10. Download via the Apple App Store

The app is distributed via the Apple App Store. When you download, buy or update it, Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland) processes data such as your Apple Account, purchase information and device data under its own responsibility. I have no influence on this.

If you have enabled “Share with App Developers” in the iOS Settings, Apple provides me with anonymized crash and usage statistics. They do not allow any conclusions about you as a person.

11. Your rights

Where I process personal data about you – for example when you send me an email – you have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR). Just send me an email.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the Thuringian State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (TLfDI).

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: rocconaut@icloud.com

If you send me an email, I process your email address and the content of your message only to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in answering requests) or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract. I delete your message once the request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. I use Apple iCloud Mail for email (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland).

13. Changes

I will update this privacy policy if the app or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
