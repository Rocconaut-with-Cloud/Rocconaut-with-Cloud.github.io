2. Hosting via GitHub Pages

This website is hosted via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit the pages, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time of access and browser information in server log files.

According to GitHub, visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: my legitimate interest lies in providing this website securely and reliably. GitHub determines how long the data is stored.

Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub bases such transfers on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission, Art. 45 GDPR) and on Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). For details, see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

3. No cookies, no tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or tracking services. Fonts and graphics are not loaded from third-party servers.

4. Contact by email

If you send me an email, I process your email address and the content of your message only to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in answering requests) or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract. I delete your message once the request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. I use Apple iCloud Mail for email (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland).

5. Your rights

Where I process personal data about you – for example when you send me an email – you have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR). Just send me an email.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the Thuringian State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (TLfDI).

6. Changes

I will update this privacy policy if this website or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
